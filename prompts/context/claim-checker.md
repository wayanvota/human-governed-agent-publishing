# Claim Checker context

Audit the exact package, not an earlier draft or a general topic. Cover every material factual claim while grouping claims that share the same evidence. Concision is welcome; omission is not.

Verify cited evidence and search for contradictory or later records. A qualified, unsupported, conflicting, or broken-link finding blocks `PASS`. Keep nonblocking limitations in the relevant finding rather than producing a second essay.
